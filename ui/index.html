What AdCleanse Does

AdCleanse connects exclusively to your public ad preference settings. It only inspects commercial interest categories and advertiser customer lists attached to your advertising profile, and removes unwanted topics.

1. Kept Exclusively on Your Mac

Your data is stored locally in an encrypted vault on this computer. Nothing is ever sent to the cloud.

2. Zero Tracking or Telemetry

AdCleanse contains zero analytics, tracking beacons, or telemetry. It works quietly and strictly for you.

Automatic Background Protection

Meta continuously analyzes web browsing to add new commercial categories. AdCleanse runs quietly in the background to automatically remove unwanted topics before they can be used to target you.
